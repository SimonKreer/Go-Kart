# Build Log

The kart was built by two people over occasional weekends with a budget of about €150.

## 1. Concept

Instead of buying a kart frame, the build started from an old Kettcar frame that was already available. This keeps cost low and turns the project into an exercise in engineering around constraints.

## 2. Engine and Drive Mounting

A 212 cc four-stroke engine (7.5 hp) was mounted on the frame. Power goes through a centrifugal clutch with a 10-tooth sprocket and a chain to a 38-tooth sprocket on the rear axle.

## 3. Rear Axle Rework

The original axle seat wore into the frame, see [problems-and-solutions.md](problems-and-solutions.md). Two pillow block bearings were added at the rear.

## 4. Current State

The kart runs. The rear axle and chain are being reworked.
